05 // BEFORE YOU SEND IT

Ask the right questions.

Can you save any bad recording?

No. Heavy distortion, missing information, or a rough performance may need a re-record. That is exactly why the preflight exists.

Do I keep my song and rights?

Yes. This is a processing service. You retain ownership of your song and the files you supply.

Is this full mixing and mastering?

No. This is a focused rescue pass. Full multitrack mix work, production, and larger mastering projects are scoped separately after review.

What should I upload?

Send your best available file, the beat if you are submitting vocals, one reference song, and a blunt note about what is bothering you.
